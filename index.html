01 / About

A manager who understands the craft.

I'm Suyash Barge, a Graphic Manager with a background spanning jewellery photography, retouching, visual design, video, catalogues and international exhibition creatives. My career has evolved from hands-on production into managing complex creative workflows.

At VD Jewels Artison Pvt Ltd, I supervise an eight-person graphics team, coordinate workloads and quality standards, and support production across domestic and export visual requirements. I also coordinate operational needs including IT troubleshooting, internet/ISP issues, equipment procurement and vendor discussions.

I explore tools such as ChatGPT, Codex, Claude, Gemini and Google Flow to support AI-based imagery, motion content, workflow improvements and software experimentation — with human review and quality control throughout.
